ICP Profile
The Multi-Location
Business Owner
You operate 2–20+ locations across a region or nationally. Each location has its own market, its own competitive set, and you're trying to run Google Ads for all of them.
Verticals HVAC, medspa, dental, home services
Spend $3K–$30K/mo per location
Primary pain Cost per lead, lead quality
Goal Qualified leads at a predictable CPL
"I'm getting leads — but my sales team says half of them aren't serious. I don't know if it's the ads or the market."
01 /
You're paying for leads. You're not paying for qualified leads.
The campaign optimizes for form fills and calls. It doesn't know which ones turned into booked appointments, closed sales, or repeat customers — because that data lives in your CRM, not in Google Ads. So the algorithm chases volume, not value.
Lead quality Offline conversion import High-value fix
Budget on bad leads
02 /
Every location is competing against itself.
When your Scottsdale and Phoenix campaigns run under the same account structure, they bid against each other in overlapping geo targets — inflating your own costs. Location consolidation without proper geo architecture is money thrown at an auction you're running against yourself.
Self-competition Geo targeting Campaign structure
CPL inflated 20–40%
03 /
Call tracking is broken or non-existent.
If your conversion is a phone call, you need to know which keyword, which ad, which geo drove that call — and whether it was answered. Most setups count the ring. Not the answer. Not the booking. Google is optimizing for phone rings from people who hung up.
Call tracking Conversion quality Healthcare compliance
Phantom conversions
04 /
Your negative keyword list hasn't been touched in months.
Broad match and PMax are serving to "near me" searches in markets where you don't operate. DIY queries. Competitor brand searches. Adjacent services you don't offer. Every irrelevant click is a dollar that didn't go toward a real prospect.
Wasted reach Negative keywords Search term hygiene
$1K–$8K/mo leaked
05 /
Seasonality hits you every year like it's new information.
HVAC in August. Medspa in December. Your busiest seasons are also when CPCs peak and competitors flood the auction. Without a seasonal bidding strategy and budget phasing built ahead of time, you're reactive — and you always overpay at the worst moment.
Seasonal planning Budget strategy Auction dynamics
Reactive = expensive
06 /
You have no single view of what's actually working across locations.
One location is crushing CPL. Two others are hemorrhaging budget. You don't have a unified reporting view that breaks down performance by location, by keyword intent, by lead stage — so decisions happen on gut, not data.
Reporting gap Looker Studio Quick win
Flying blind

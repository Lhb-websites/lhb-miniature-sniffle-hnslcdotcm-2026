Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a paid acquisition systems operator actually do?

A systems operator builds and owns the full technical infrastructure behind Google Ads — campaign architecture, conversion tracking, server-side tagging, first-party data pipelines, and ongoing optimization. Unlike an agency, there are no account managers in the middle. You get direct operator access and accountability for outcomes.

What verticals do you specialize in?

eCommerce and Shopping (Google Shopping, PMax, feed optimization), healthcare and medspa (HIPAA-compliant structure, lead quality tracking), B2B SaaS (pipeline attribution, CRM integration), and lead generation (high-intent search, aggressive CPA management for legal, finance, and home services).

Do you only work with US-based businesses?

Yes. The focus is exclusively US-market businesses — the competitive dynamics, platform behavior, and bid landscapes are different enough that full specialization is the only way to deliver consistently.

How is this different from hiring a Google Ads agency?

Most agencies assign an account manager who oversees a specialist who runs the campaigns. Performance depends on whoever happens to be assigned. Here, Hans designs and manages the system personally, with pod-level continuity built in so performance is never dependent on a single person leaving.

What does "bulletproof tracking" mean in practice?

It means your conversion data is accurate, complete, and resistant to browser restrictions and ad blockers — using server-side tagging, enhanced conversions, offline conversion imports, and first-party data pipelines. When your tracking is clean, every optimization decision you make is based on reality.
